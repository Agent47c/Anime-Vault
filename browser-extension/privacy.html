Privacy & data use

Local storage and explicit access

The extension stores your chosen website, enabled state and connection status in Chrome's local extension storage. It does not collect browsing history or send analytics to an Anime Vault telemetry service.

Your selected website

Capture scripts run on the website you explicitly enable. Searches requested by the desktop app may open an inactive tab on that source, read its release-result links and close the temporary tab. The extension does not inspect unrelated websites. Disabling capture or revoking the source permission stops this integration.

Communication

Selected magnet links and search results are passed to your local Anime Vault application using Chrome native messaging. Search queries are sent to your configured website. The desktop app separately contacts metadata providers and, when you approve downloads, torrent trackers and peers. Those services have their own privacy practices and may receive your IP address.

Your desktop data and backups

Anime Vault stores library paths, watch state, notes, download records and caches locally. Optional backups can contain private library information and source/transfer metadata. Protect backup files and redact logs before sharing them. The extension does not upload your library database or anime files.

Permissions and controls

Storage saves preferences; scripting enables the selected-site integration; native messaging connects the desktop bridge; alarms support reconnection. Broad host patterns in the manifest are optional choices, not automatic access to every site. Review granted permissions in Chrome.

This policy describes the current app and extension behavior. No new analytics or remote collection was added as part of the UI redesign.
